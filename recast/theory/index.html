    Theory¶

    The equations of the Methods of the paper and those of its Supplementary Information that the code implements (numbers S1, S2, ...), with their numbers there, in the notation of the code. The strain of the cell is the Green-Lagrange strain E. Each equation links to the function that implements it; the comment directly above that function states the same equation, and tools/check_equations.py checks that it does.

    Deformation and targets¶

    (1) Deformation of the calculated structure¶

    L_hat = L U, f_hat = f + u L^-1, U = (I + 2E)^(1/2), so that r_hat_k = (r_k + u_k) U

    Implemented by recast.predict.corrected.

    L cell rows (angstrom), f fractional coordinates, r_k = f_k L, E a symmetric Green-Lagrange strain, u_k displacements (angstrom). U is the symmetric stretch, defined when lambda_min(I + 2E) > 0.

    (2) Strain target¶

    E = 1/2 (L_ref^-1 G_tgt L_ref^-T - I), G_tgt = L_tgt L_tgt^T

    Implemented by recast.geometry.green.

    With L_ref the calculated cell and L_tgt the experimental one this is E, so that L_tgt = L_ref (I + 2E)^(1/2) R_0 with R_0 orthogonal and det(I + 2E)^(1/2) = V/V.

    (3) Displacement target¶

    u*_k = (f_e,k - f_c,k) L_c - (1/n) sum_l (f_e,l - f_c,l) L_c

    Implemented by recast.targets.displacement_target.

    f_c are the calculated fractional coordinates unwrapped to the image nearest the experimental ones f_e.

    Error measures¶

    (4) Error measures¶

    e_V = |J_hat / J* - 1|, e_S = |S(E_hat) - S(E*)|_F,
e_L = (1/3) sum_i |(G_hat_ii / G*_ii)^(1/2) - 1|, e_A = (1/3) sum_{j<k} |phi_jk(G_hat) - phi_jk(G*)|,
e_B = (1/|B|) sum_b |ln(d_hat_b / d*_b)|, e_D = ((1/n') sum_{k not H} |u_hat_k - u*_k - t|^2)^(1/2)

    Implemented by recast.errors.structure_errors.

    J = det(I + 2E)^(1/2), S(E) = 1/2 ln(J^(-2/3) (I + 2E)) the shape, G = L (I + 2E) L^T, phi_jk the cell angles, B the first-shell bonds of the experimental structure between atoms other than hydrogen, t the mean of u_hat_k - u*_k over the n' atoms other than hydrogen.

    Input features¶

    (5) eSEN-30M-OMat descriptors averaged with the inversion image¶

    x_k = [s_k(S) + s_k(I S)] / 2

    Implemented by recast.esen.descriptors.

    For the variant of the cell estimators. s_k the l = 0 rows of the node embedding of atom k after each of the ten blocks (1,280 values), I S the inversion image of the structure S (fractional coordinates -f mod 1, same cell).

    (6) Hessian on the retained modes¶

    S H S = U diag(w) U^T, S = diag(g_K / V^(1/3), I); H_ret = S^-1 U_k diag(w_k) U_k^T S^-1 over w_k > TOL

    Implemented by recast.features.retained.

    g_K = (1, 1, 1, sqrt 2, sqrt 2, sqrt 2); (w_k, U_k) the eigenpairs of S H S; TOL = 1e-3 eV/angstrom^2. H is the exact Green-Lagrange Hessian of MACE-OMAT-0 (equation S2); H_ret has zero stiffness along the removed modes.

    Atom and pair networks¶

    (7) Pair targets and weights¶

    s_p = 1e-2 o_0, w_p = f_c(d_p) exp(clip(o_1, -3, 3)), (o_0, o_1) = MLP_p(z_p)

    Implemented by recast.networks.DLSNet.forward.

    s_p is a target Green strain of pair p, (d'^2 / d^2 - 1) / 2; z_p the invariant pair features (equation S5).

    Strain estimators¶

    (8) Direct strain head (estimator 1)¶

    E_d = 1e-2 [t I / 3 + (1/n) sum_i (a_i T_i + c_i dev(T_i T_i))] + sum_k m_k [c_k dev T_k + d_k tr(T_k) I / 3],
T_i = sum over the pairs p containing i of w_p dev(r_hat_p r_hat_p^T)

    Implemented by recast.networks.StrainNet.forward.

    (a_i, c_i), t and (c_k, d_k) are outputs of networks on invariants; w_p the bond weights of the head; T_k the Green-Lagrange relaxation strains (equation S3) and m_k their masks.

    (9) Linearized Green strain of a pair¶

    a_p . q = r_hat_p^T E r_hat_p + r_hat_p . (u_j - u_i) / d_p

    Implemented by recast.fits.pair_rows.

    q = (gamma, u), gamma the engineering Voigt vector of E; a_p is the row of pair p; exact at u = 0.

    (10) Distance-least-squares fit (estimators 2 and 3)¶

    (A + Lam) q = r, Lam = diag(lam_g RG, lam_u I)

    Implemented by recast.fits.solve_free.

    A = sum_p w_p a_p a_p^T and r = sum_p w_p s_p a_p (recast.fits.normal_eq), RG = diag(1, 1, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2); estimator 2 solves for the strain alone (u = 0).

    (11) Missing energy of the pair restraints¶

    Delta E(q) = kappa0 / 2 sum_p w_p (a_p . q - s_p)^2 = q^T A q / 2 - r . q + const

    Implemented by recast.fits.normal_eq.

    A and r computed with the spring constants kappa0 w_p.

    (12) Hessian-regularized distance-least-squares fit (estimator 4)¶

    M q = kappa_0 b, M = P H P + kappa_0 (A + Lam_0)

    Implemented by recast.estimators.Member.solve.

    P removes the three rigid translations from the Hessian H (equation 6, float64); Lam_0 the ridge term of equation 10.

    (13) Hessian-regularized fit with a strain prior (estimator 5)¶

    (P H P + K A + Lam_d + Lam) q = K r + Lam_d q_d

    Implemented by recast.networks.UnifiedNet.forward.

    K = kappa exp(z_K), Lam_d = diag(lam_V P_V + lam_S P_S, 0) with lam_V, lam_S = K (sum_p w_p) exp(z_V, z_S), q_d = (gamma(E_d), 0), (z_V, z_S, z_K) = 4 tanh(o / 4).

    (14) Mean strain¶

    E_hat = (1 / M) sum_m E_m

    Implemented by recast.estimators.mean_strain.

    The arithmetic mean over all cell members, equal to the mean over the five estimators of their means over the initializations; a mean with lambda_min(I + 2E_hat) <= 1e-6 has no cell and returns the calculated structure.

    Atomic positions¶

    (15) Atoms at a given cell¶

    u = (A_uu + lam_u I)^-1 (r_u - A_ug gamma_c)

    Implemented by recast.fits.solve_clamped.

    A and r of the Hessian-regularized fit (equation 12), so that A_uu + lam_u I = M_uu and r_u = kappa_0 b_u.

    Input features¶

    (S2) Green-Lagrange Hessian¶

    H = d2W/dq2 at q = 0 = [[K, Lam^T], [Lam, Phi]],
W(q) the energy of the cell L U and positions (r + u) U, U = (I + 2E(gamma))^(1/2), by automatic differentiation

    Implemented by recast.features.mace_hessian.

    K = V C0 (eV) the strain block, Lam (eV/angstrom) the internal-strain coupling, Phi (eV/angstrom^2) the force constants, all in float64; recast.features.hessian_fd gives the same matrix by central differences for any ASE calculator.

    (S3) Relaxation strains¶

    T1 = E(L, L_1), T0 = E(L, L_0), D = T1 - T0

    Implemented by recast.features.relaxation_tensors.

    E(L, L') = 1/2 (L^-1 L' L'^T L^-T - I); L_0 and L_1 the cells relaxed with MACE-OMAT-0 without and with D3; the networks read T1 and D, each with a mask that is 1 when its relaxations converged.

    Atom and pair networks¶

    (S4) Channel quadrupoles of the atoms¶

    Q_i^k = sum over the pairs p containing i of f_k(p) dev(r_hat_p r_hat_p^T),
f_k(p) = MLP_q(h_i + h_j, h_i * h_j, rbf(d_p)) f_c(d_p)

    Implemented by recast.networks.DLSNet.quadrupoles.

    h_i the embedded descriptors, rbf 16 Gaussians, f_c the cosine cutoff at 5 angstrom, k = 1..8.

    (S5) Invariant features of a pair¶

    z_p = (h_i + h_j, h_i * h_j, rbf(d_p), {o_i^k + o_j^k, o_i^k o_j^k, tr(Q_i^k Q_j^k),
r_hat_p^T (Q_i^k Q_j^k + Q_j^k Q_i^k) r_hat_p / 2}_k, chi_U, g, {100 m_k r_hat_p^T T_k r_hat_p, 100 m_k tr T_k,
m_k}_k), o_i^k = r_hat_p^T Q_i^k r_hat_p

    Implemented by recast.networks.DLSNet.pair_features.

    chi_U the Hubbard-U flag, g the structure mean of h_i, T_k and m_k the relaxation strains and their masks.

    Training and model selection¶

    (S6) Cell loss¶

    joint rho(s2 + t2) or decoupled rho(s2) + rho(t2), mean over the batch, with s2 = |S(E_hat) - S(E*)|_F^2,
t2 = w0 (ln J(E_hat) - ln J(E*))^2 / 3, rho(x) = delta^2 (sqrt(1 + x / delta^2) - 1)

    Implemented by recast.losses.cell_loss.

    The loss of the error measures themselves (S the shape, ln J the log volume ratio), evaluated in float64 by a Cayley transform and the atanh series (recast.losses); a prediction outside the domain gets the quadratic form of the same loss at its target.

    (S7) Atom loss¶

    rho(L_g + wb L_b + wu sum um (q - q*)^2)

    Implemented by recast.losses.atom_loss.

    L_b the weighted mean squared linearized bond-length error over the first-shell bonds without hydrogen, um = 1 / (n' dbar^2) on the atoms other than hydrogen; at the experimental cell L_g = 0.

    (S8) Corrected resampled t-test¶

    t = dbar / sqrt((1/J + n2/n1) s_d^2)

    Implemented by recast.score.corrected_t.

    d_j the paired fold differences, J folds, n2 and n1 the test and training sizes; J - 1 degrees of freedom.
